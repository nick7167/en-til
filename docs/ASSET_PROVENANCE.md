# Artwork and fonts

Six approved reference sheets are copied without modification to design/references. They are visual references, not rendered application screens.

`ios/EnTil/Assets.xcassets/Lounge.imageset/lounge.png` was generated with the built-in image-generation tool on 10 September 2026 using the first approved sheet as a style/character reference. Prompt: production square illustration of the approved late-night lounge, relaxed lime arch-shaped character on violet sofa with coral round, lavender angular and peach shy friends, indigo foliage, shaded warm lamp, snack bowl and oval table; preserve silhouettes/faces/colors; ink #10101E, top third open; no text, controls, phone frame or watermark. Original retained in Codex generated_images/01a08935-5b1c-7b30-9eb8-298443c5a11b.

Native character drawings are scalable SwiftUI Canvas artwork matching the twelve approved silhouette/color concepts. Final expression/pose and per-screen artwork review remains required. Generated art is an implementation candidate, not new user approval.

Six pack covers are saved under `ios/EnTil/Assets.xcassets/Pack-*.imageset/cover.png` and used in shop/setup/detail views. Full prompts are in PACK_ART_PROMPTS.md; all were generated through the built-in tool, inspected, and copied into the project. Fraunces is vendored from the official Google Fonts ofl/fraunces directory with its OFL license in Resources. Synthesized lock/reveal/ready cues are generated by scripts/make-sounds.py; no sampled third-party audio.
