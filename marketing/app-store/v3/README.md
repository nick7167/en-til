# App Store creative v3

This is the rebuilt direction after review. It removes all inset app screenshots, device-like cards, numbered labels, and generic feature copy. The six cards are pure promotional key art: full scenes, complete characters, speech bubbles, physical question/score cards, and game-specific moments.

The copy follows the actual loop: know the group, answer secretly, back a friend, move two fields, race to the finish, and play another round. The visual approach was informed by the current App Store creative for [Heads Up!](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/heads-up/id623592465) and [Psych!](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/psych-outwit-your-friends/id1005765746): bold benefit hooks, in-world speech bubbles, human/social reaction, and a clear visual idea per card.

The six files are 1320 × 2868 px JPGs for the 6.9-inch iPhone slot. They are drafts only and are not connected to the Xcode project or App Store Connect.
