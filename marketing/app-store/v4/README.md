# App Store creative v4

This pass keeps the social story from v3 but adds the missing polish: saturated gradient fields, glossy rounded bubbles, sticker-style character outlines, soft depth shadows, badges, confetti, and stronger scale contrast. There are no app screenshots or device frames.

The scenes use game-specific moments and copy: the planet question, secret answers, choosing Freja/Noah/Alma, moving two fields, the last question, and the rematch invitation. Characters are fully framed and staged as a group rather than clipped at the edges.

The six files are 1320 × 2868 px JPGs for the 6.9-inch iPhone slot. These remain drafts and are not uploaded or wired into the app listing.
