# App Store creative draft

These are six Danish, portrait promotional cards for the App Store product page. They tell the game story in order: gather, answer, back a friend, reveal points, race to the finish, and rematch.

Files `app-store-01.jpg` through `app-store-06.jpg` are 1320 × 2868 px, suitable for the 6.9-inch iPhone screenshot slot. The cards combine the approved lounge/character artwork with real native capture panels from the latest visual checkpoint. Copy is deliberately short and benefit-led for quick scanning.

`icon-concept-01.png` through `icon-concept-03.png` are opaque 1024 × 1024 App Store icon candidates. The current app icon is unchanged. Concept 02 is the strongest small-size candidate: three characters, one question badge, and a high-contrast dark field. Concepts 01 and 03 are alternatives for comparison at 60 px.

These are drafts only. They are not yet wired into the Xcode asset catalog or uploaded to App Store Connect.
