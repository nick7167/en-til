# App Store creative v5

This pass follows the modern App Store pattern visible in Duolingo’s current listing: the native product view fills the canvas, while one oversized rounded color panel carries a short, benefit-led message. It uses SF rounded typography, bright brand colors, soft depth, and no decorative poster labels or inset mockups.

The six cards use the real En til? capture sequence: home, private question, backing, board movement, reveal/finale, and lobby. Copy is specific to each mechanic and remains readable at a glance.

Files are 1320 × 2868 px JPGs for the 6.9-inch iPhone slot. They are drafts only and are not uploaded or wired into the App Store listing.
